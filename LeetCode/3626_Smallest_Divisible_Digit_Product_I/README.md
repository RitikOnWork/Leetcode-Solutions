# 3626. Smallest Divisible Digit Product I

## 🔗 Original Problem

[LeetCode - Smallest Divisible Digit Product I](https://leetcode.com/problems/smallest-divisible-digit-product-i/)

---

## 📝 Problem Statement

You are given two integers `n` and `t`. Return the **smallest** number greater than or equal to `n` such that the **product of its digits** is divisible by `t`.

---

## 💡 Examples

**Example 1:**

<div class="example-block">

**Input:** <span class="example-io">n = 10, t = 2</span>

**Output:** <span class="example-io">10</span>

**Explanation:**

The digit product of 10 is 0, which is divisible by 2, making it the smallest number greater than or equal to 10 that satisfies the condition.
</div>

**Example 2:**

<div class="example-block">

**Input:** <span class="example-io">n = 15, t = 3</span>

**Output:** <span class="example-io">16</span>

**Explanation:**

The digit product of 16 is 6, which is divisible by 3, making it the smallest number greater than or equal to 15 that satisfies the condition.
</div>

---

## 📌 Constraints

</strong>

	- `1 <= n <= 100`

	- `1 <= t <= 10`
